Get the apps

Download a build, then check it: the SHA-256 of the file you got must match the one below (Windows: certutil -hashfile <file> SHA256; Mac: shasum -a 256 <file>).

How to get started

  1. Download and install the app for your phone or computer below.
  2. Sign in with your email and password or with Google. No account yet? Choose Create account when this server offers it, or ask whoever runs it to invite you.
  3. Run the Ready check the app opens with, fix anything it shows with one tap, then record a call or a voice note. Your recordings are private to you until you share them.

No builds are published yet.

What works where

  • Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel), macOS 14.4 or newer: voice notes, imports and both sides of calls. The app does not open on older versions.
  • Windows 11: both sides of calls, hearing only the call's app.
  • Windows 10: both sides of calls, but it records all sound playing on the computer.
  • Android 14 or newer with the capture helper: both sides of app calls.
  • Android 12 and 13 with the capture helper: only your voice in app calls.
  • Android 10 and 11, or no helper: voice notes and imports, no calls: during a call Android gives other apps no sound.
  • Google Play version: voice notes and imports, no calls.

Tested so far on phones: Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra. In the app, Help & diagnostics → Device check says what your phone can do.
